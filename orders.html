Questions

Good to know

How do I place an order?

Send us the name of the piece on Instagram or Facebook. We’ll confirm availability and everything else in the chat.

How do payment and delivery work?

Once we confirm your order, we’ll share the payment and delivery options available for your area.

Can I see the pieces in person?

Yes — we regularly take part in markets and festivals in Cyprus. Check our events and follow us on Instagram for the next one.

How do I care for my jewelry?

Wipe your pieces with a soft cloth after wearing, and store them separately to avoid scratches. Keep them away from perfume and harsh chemicals to preserve their shine.
